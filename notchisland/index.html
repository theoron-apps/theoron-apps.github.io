NotchIsland

Dynamic Island for MacBooks with a notch

Your notch becomes a living island: music, notifications, timers, files and your coding agents, right where your eyes already are.

01Music

Every song, right in the notch.

Music and video from any player, not just Apple Music and Spotify: YouTube in Safari, Chrome and Brave, podcasts, VLC, IINA, Telegram songs, even FL Studio while it plays.

  • Controls in the island: title, time, seeking, play and skip.
  • A live equalizer in four styles that reacts to real sound, and a glow around the island.
  • The video frame and channel with the NotchIsland browser extension.

Go ahead: play, skip and seek.

02AI agents

Your coding agents, at a glance.

See what Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and Antigravity are doing while you work in another window.

  • At work: the project and the current action, right under the notch.
  • Waiting for you: the notch pulses and taps the trackpad when an agent needs permission.
  • Agents Office: a cutaway room where every agent sits at its project’s desk.
  • Limits for Claude, Codex and Cursor, with reset times and warnings.

When it asks, click the island to approve.

03Notifications

Small news, in the right place.

The island tells you what just happened, then shrinks back into the notch.

  • Charging
  • AirPods and Bluetooth
  • Network and VPN
  • Another Mac over Thunderbolt
  • High temperature
  • Bright screen on battery
  • Volume

04Activities

Up to three things at once.

A timer, Keep Awake, camera and microphone indicators and calls stay beside the island, in one of six styles.

  • Timer with a ring that empties as time runs out.
  • Keep Awake: your Mac stays awake for as long as you need.
  • Camera and microphone: always know when they are on.

In the app, right-click the island for a quick menu: timer, Keep Awake, shelf and more.

05Stats and battery

Your Mac’s vital signs, one swipe away.

CPU, memory, GPU, disk, network, temperatures, power draw in watts and screen brightness in nits. Click Memory to see what is using it, and quit apps right from the island.

New in 6.1

Fan speed limit

Hover over the fan ring and scroll: 100 rpm steps, from Auto down to full silence. Above a temperature you choose, the fans go back to automatic on their own.

Apple silicon Macs with fans.

Try it here: hover over the Fans ring and scroll.

First launch

  1. Download the DMG and drag NotchIsland into Applications.
  2. Open it. macOS says it can’t verify the developer: click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and scroll down to Security.
  4. Click Open Anyway and confirm with your password or Touch ID.
  5. Launch NotchIsland again. A short tour shows you around.

The app isn’t signed with a paid Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS asks you to confirm the first launch once.
